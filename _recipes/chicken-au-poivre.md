---
layout: recipe
title: Chicken au Poivre
description: A spatchcocked chicken roasted in a peppercorn, garlic, rosemary, and cream sauce finished with sherry.
prep_time: 20 minutes
cook_time: 50 minutes
servings: 4-6
category: Dinner
protein: chicken
source: Coco Larkin Cooks

ingredients: |
  - 1 whole roasting chicken, about 4½ pounds
  - 1 tablespoon Maldon salt, divided, for the chicken
  - ½ teaspoon olive oil
  - 1½ tablespoons whole peppercorns
  - 1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh rosemary
  - 5 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 1 medium to large shallot, finely chopped
  - 1 teaspoon Maldon salt, for the cream mixture
  - 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  - ½ cup dry sherry cooking wine

instructions: |
  1. Spatchcock the chicken by cutting along both sides of the backbone so it lies flat. Your butcher can do this for you.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (convection).
  3. Season the underside of the chicken with ½ tablespoon of the salt. Rub the skin side with the olive oil, then season with the remaining ½ tablespoon salt.
  4. Crush the peppercorns between two tea towels with a mallet or rolling pin, or use a mortar and pestle. Leave them coarse, with some larger pieces.
  5. In a bowl, combine the crushed peppercorns, rosemary, garlic, shallot, and 1 teaspoon salt. Whisk in the heavy cream and set aside.
  6. Place the chicken skin-side up in the oven and roast for about 15 minutes, until slightly golden.
  7. Remove the chicken and pour the peppercorn cream over it. Add the sherry to the cream mixture, pouring some around the chicken and some into the sauce.
  8. Raise the oven temperature to 400°F and return the chicken to the oven. Roast for 30-35 minutes, basting about every 15 minutes, until golden and bubbling and an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part of the breast reads 165°F.
  9. Let the chicken rest for at least 10 minutes before serving. Serve with mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or boiled new potatoes, and a baguette for soaking up the sauce.

notes: |
  - Spatchcocking makes the chicken cook faster and more evenly.
  - If your chicken is spatchcocked with the backbone removed, start it breast-side down in the cream mixture for the first 15 minutes, then flip it breast-side up to finish.
