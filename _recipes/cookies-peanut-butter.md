---
layout: recipe
title: Peanut Butter Cookies
prep_time: 25 minutes
cook_time: 8 minutes per batch
temperature: 375°F
servings: 6 dozen
category: Dessert
source: Jamie Williams
flavor: peanut butter

ingredients: |
  - 1 cup butter, softened
  - 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 1 cup firmly packed brown sugar
  - 2 large eggs
  - 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  - 2 teaspoons baking soda
  - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - additional sugar for fork-dipping

instructions: |
  1. Beat butter and peanut butter at medium speed with an electric mixer until creamy. Gradually add sugars, beating well. Add eggs, beating well.
  2. In a medium bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt. Add to butter mixture, beating well. Stir in vanilla.
  3. Cover and chill for 3 hours.
  4. Shape dough into 1½-inch balls. Place 3 inches apart on ungreased cookie sheets.
  5. Dip a fork in additional sugar and flatten each cookie in a criss-cross pattern.
  6. Bake at 375°F for 7 to 8 minutes.
  7. Remove to wire racks to cool.
