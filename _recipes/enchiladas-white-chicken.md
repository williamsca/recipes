---
layout: recipe
title: White Chicken Enchiladas
description: Flour tortillas filled with shredded chicken and cheese, smothered in a creamy green chile white sauce and baked until bubbly.
prep_time: 15 minutes
cook_time: 25 minutes
servings: 5
category: Dinner
protein: chicken
source: Kristi Terasa

ingredients: |
  **Filling:**
  - 2 cups shredded, cooked chicken (can use a store-bought rotisserie chicken)
  - 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese (or mozzarella)
  - salt, pepper, Adobo seasoning, to taste
  - 10 flour tortillas (taco size)

  **Sauce:**
  - 3 Tablespoons butter
  - 3 Tablespoons all-purpose flour
  - 2 cups chicken broth
  - 1 cup sour cream
  - 4 ounce can diced green chiles (do not drain)

  **Topping:**
  - 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese

instructions: |
  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Combine the shredded chicken and 1 cup of Monterey Jack in a bowl. Season with salt, pepper, and Adobo seasoning to taste.
  3. Divide the filling among the tortillas, then roll each one up and arrange seam-side down in the baking dish.
  4. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and let it cook for 1 minute until it thickens slightly — keep it moving so it doesn't brown.
  5. Pour in the chicken broth and whisk until smooth.
  6. Stir in the sour cream and the entire can of green chiles (liquid included). Keep the heat low enough that the sauce doesn't boil.
  7. Remove from heat and pour the sauce evenly over the rolled enchiladas.
  8. Scatter the remaining cup of Monterey Jack over the top.
  9. Bake 20–25 minutes until heated through. For a browned, bubbly top, switch the broiler to high for a minute or two at the end.
