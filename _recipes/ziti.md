layout: recipe
title: Baked Ziti with Sausage
prep_time: 30 minutes
cook_time: 25 minutes
temperature: 350°F
servings: 8
source: Southern Living
category: Dinner
effort: medium
duration: hours
protein: pork

ingredients: |
  - 12 oz. uncooked ziti pasta
  - 4 oz. pancetta or bacon, diced
  - 1 large onion, chopped
  - 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  - 1 lb. ground Italian sausage
  - 1 cup dry red wine
  - 1 (28-oz.) can crushed tomatoes
  - 1/2 cup torn fresh basil, firmly packed
  - 1/2 tsp. kosher salt
  - 1/2 tsp. dried crushed red pepper
  - 1 cup ricotta cheese
  - 1 (8-oz.) pkg. shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  - Vegetable cooking spray
  - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

instructions: |
  1. **Cook the pasta:** Preheat oven to 350°F. Prepare ziti according to package directions for al dente.
  2. **Cook the meat sauce:** Meanwhile, cook pancetta in a large skillet over medium-high heat for 3 minutes. Add onion and garlic, and sauté 3 minutes or until onion is tender. Add sausage, and sauté 5 minutes or until meat is no longer pink. Add wine, and cook 3 minutes. Stir in tomatoes, basil, salt, and red pepper. Reduce heat to low, and cook, stirring occasionally, 3 minutes.
  3. **Assemble the dish:** Stir ricotta and 1 cup mozzarella into the hot cooked pasta. Lightly grease a 13x9-inch baking dish with cooking spray. Transfer pasta mixture to the prepared dish. Top with sausage mixture. Sprinkle with Parmesan and remaining 1 cup mozzarella.
  4. **Bake:** Bake uncovered at 350°F for 25 to 30 minutes, or until bubbly.

notes: |
  - Bake uncovered so the top browns lightly as it heats through.
  - If the ziti comes out dry, the pasta may have been overcooked or overbaked — pull it as soon as it's bubbling.
